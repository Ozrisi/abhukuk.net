Frequently asked questions

A clear
first step.

How can I arrange a first meeting?+

You can call our office or email us. The timing and format of the meeting are agreed together. A brief description of the subject is sufficient for the initial contact.

Which documents should I bring?+

You may prepare relevant agreements, correspondence, notices and case details. The preparation section provides an example list for each subject. Specific requirements become clearer once your matter has been reviewed.

How are the scope of work and fees agreed?+

The nature and extent of the work and the circumstances of the matter are considered. The scope of services, fees and possible expenses are discussed before an engagement.

How will I receive updates about my matter?+

The means of communication during the engagement is agreed at the meeting. If you contact us about an existing matter, providing its reference number or subject helps us direct your enquiry.

Does this website provide legal advice?+

No. The content provides general information about our practice areas and how to contact us. A legal assessment of a specific matter requires a separate review of the facts and documents.
